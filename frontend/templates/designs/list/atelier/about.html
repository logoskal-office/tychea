ATELIÉR isn't just a fragrance house; it's an obsidian atélier where memories are distilled and emotions are bottled.

Our Olfactory Heritage

Founded in the pursuit of olfactory perfection, ATELIÉR emerged from a single vision: to create fragrances that act as invisible architectures of the self. Our journey began in 1994, sourcing rare resins from the Arabian Peninsula and delicate petals from the valleys of Grasse.

We believe that a scent is the most intense form of memory. Every collection we release is a chapter in an ongoing story of elegance, power, and intimacy.
