Aurelius Leather Chair

$4,850

A masterpiece of reductive design, the Aurelius balances imposing structural presence with a soft, curated tactile experience.

A Symphony of Form

Engineered in our Milanese atelier, the Aurelius Leather Chair represents the pinnacle of Italian craftsmanship. Every hide is hand-selected for its natural grain and deep color consistency, then oil-tanned to achieve a finish that is both incredibly soft and exceptionally durable.

The obsidian leather, sourced from the foothills of the Alps, is paired with a hand-polished gilt frame, creating a striking contrast that defines the Obsidian & Gilt philosophy: raw power meeting delicate elegance.
