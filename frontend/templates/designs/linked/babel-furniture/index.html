How a piece gets made

From rough board to finished piece

Board Selection

We hand-pick every board for grain and stability before it ever touches a blade.

Milling & Joinery

Mortise-and-tenon, dovetail, and draw-bore joints — cut for strength that outlasts screws and staples.

Sanding & Fit

Progressive hand-sanding and a dry fit of every joint before glue-up begins.

Oil & Finish

Hardwax oil or hand-rubbed finish, applied in coats, cured before it ever leaves the shop.

The workshop

Three people. One bench. No factory.

Hearthwood & Co. is a small joinery shop — not a warehouse. Every chair, bed, table, and cabinet is built to order by the same three woodworkers who answer your emails, which means we can talk through wood species, dimensions, and finish before a single board is cut.

We keep our order book small on purpose. It's the only way to hand-fit every joint and stand behind every piece that leaves the shop.
