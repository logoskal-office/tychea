THE PRACTICE

A family clinic, built by a family of specialists.

Family Dental Specialty Clinic opened its doors in Bole in 2011 with a simple idea: Addis Ababa families shouldn't have to cross the city between a pediatric dentist, an orthodontist, and a surgeon. Today, twelve specialists share one building, one patient record, and one standard of care — so your whole household can be seen under a single roof, in Amharic, English, or Afaan Oromo.

We still believe the best dentistry feels unhurried. Every visit starts with a conversation, not a drill.

Dr. Selamawit Bekele

Founder & Chief Prosthodontist

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a referral to see a specialist? +

No. Any patient can book directly with the specialist of their choice, though our front desk is glad to help you find the right fit if you're unsure.

What age do you accept children from? +

Our pediatric team welcomes a child's first visit from around age one, in a room designed to feel nothing like a clinic.

How far in advance should I book? +

Routine checkups: 3–5 days. Specialist consultations: 1–2 weeks is ideal, though we hold same-day slots for urgent needs.

Do you offer payment plans for major treatment? +

Yes — implants, orthodontics, and full smile makeovers can be split into monthly installments arranged with our billing office.
