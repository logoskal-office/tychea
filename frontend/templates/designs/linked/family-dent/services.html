SERVICES & TREATMENTS

Every specialty your smile could need, under one roof.

From a six-month cleaning to a full smile reconstruction — our specialists coordinate your care so nothing falls through the cracks.

General Orthodontics Pediatric Oral Surgery Cosmetic Periodontics Endodontics Implants

01 — GENERAL & FAMILY DENTISTRY

The foundation of a healthy smile.

Routine care for every member of the household — cleanings, exams, and fillings delivered with a gentle, unhurried touch.

  • —Routine cleanings & exams
  • —Tooth-colored fillings
  • —Digital X-ray diagnostics
  • —Fluoride & sealant treatments
  • —Nightguards for teeth grinding
  • —Emergency same-day visits

02 — ORTHODONTICS

Straighter teeth, at any age.

Whether it's a first set of braces or an adult correcting a lifelong bite issue, our orthodontic team plans every case around your lifestyle.

  • —Traditional metal braces
  • —Ceramic (tooth-colored) braces
  • —Clear aligner therapy
  • —Early interceptive treatment for kids
  • —Retainers & long-term bite maintenance
  • —Jaw alignment consultation

03 — PEDIATRIC DENTISTRY

Building good habits from the very first tooth.

Our children's wing is designed to feel nothing like a clinic — playful, patient, and paced entirely around your child's comfort.

  • —First-visit & toddler checkups
  • —Cavity prevention & sealants
  • —Gentle cavity treatment
  • —Space maintainers
  • —Habit counseling (thumb-sucking, etc.)
  • —Sports mouthguards

04 — ORAL & MAXILLOFACIAL SURGERY

Advanced surgical care, close to home.

From wisdom teeth to jaw reconstruction, our surgical suite is equipped for procedures many Addis patients previously traveled abroad for.

  • —Wisdom tooth extraction
  • —Complex & surgical extractions
  • —Bone grafting
  • —Corrective jaw surgery
  • —Biopsy & pathology referral
  • —Sedation dentistry options

05 — COSMETIC & AESTHETIC DENTISTRY

Confidence, designed carefully.

Every smile makeover starts with a digital preview, so you know exactly what to expect before any work begins.

  • —Porcelain veneers
  • —Professional teeth whitening
  • —Bonding & contouring
  • —Full smile makeovers
  • —Gum contouring
  • —Digital smile preview

06 — PERIODONTICS

Healthy gums, the foundation beneath every smile.

Gum disease is treatable at every stage — our periodontists focus on stopping it early and keeping it from coming back.

  • —Deep cleaning (scaling & root planing)
  • —Gum disease treatment
  • —Gum grafting
  • —Crown lengthening
  • —Ongoing periodontal maintenance
  • —Pre-implant gum evaluation

07 — ENDODONTICS

Saving natural teeth, comfortably.

Modern root canal therapy is faster and gentler than most patients expect — our endodontists specialize in exactly this.

  • —Root canal therapy
  • —Retreatment of prior root canals
  • —Cracked tooth diagnosis
  • —Pulp therapy for children
  • —Post & core restoration
  • —Emergency pain relief

08 — DENTAL IMPLANTS

A permanent answer to missing teeth.

From a single tooth to a full-arch restoration, our implant team plans every case with 3D imaging for precision placement.

  • —Single-tooth implants
  • —Implant-supported bridges
  • —Full-arch restoration
  • —Implant-supported dentures
  • —3D-guided placement
  • —Bone density evaluation
