Common questions

How long does a custom order take?

Ready-made pieces in stock ship within 3–5 days. Custom finishes, sizes, or full commissions typically take 6–8 weeks, depending on the piece and current workshop load — we'll give you a firm date when you order.

Do you deliver outside Addis Ababa?

Yes — we regularly deliver to Adama, Hawassa, Bahir Dar and Dire Dawa. Delivery is free within Addis Ababa on orders over ETB 25,000; out-of-city delivery is quoted based on distance.

What's your warranty and repair policy?

Every piece carries a 3-year structural warranty covering joinery and frames. Cane re-weaving and re-oiling are available as a paid service at any point — we keep a record of every piece we've made, so repairs match the original.

Can I commission a fully custom piece?

Yes — bring a sketch, a photo, or just a room's dimensions. We start with a sit-down at the showroom to talk through wood, weave and finish, followed by a quote before any work begins.

Do you offer trade pricing for hotels or offices?

Yes — we work with hotels, restaurants and offices across the city on volume pricing and phased delivery. Select "Trade / bulk order" in the form and a project manager will follow up.
