The Entoto Lounge Chair takes its low seat height and wide arms from the reading chairs found in older Addis Ababa sitting rooms, rebuilt in solid Zigba wood with a hand-woven cane seat and backrest. Each chair is joined with mortise-and-tenon construction — no screws into end grain, no particle board — and finished with a hard-wax oil that deepens with age. Woven by the same artisans who make our dining chairs, so the weave pattern matches across a full room set.
