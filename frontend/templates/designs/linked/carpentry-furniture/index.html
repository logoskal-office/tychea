How a piece is made

From timber yard to your living room, in four stages

01

Select the design

Our raw materials are sourced and graded at our workshop then prepared for fabrication.

02

Cut & join

Frames are cut and joined by precise modern machinery — no particle board, no staples, mortise-and-tenon throughout.

03

Hand-weave

Seats and backs are woven in natural cane by artisans trained with modern techniques.

04

Finish & inspect

Oil finishing, brass hardware fitting, and a final quality pass before the piece reaches you.
