Our story

From a single bench in Merkato

Phoenix began as a two-person cabinet shop near Merkato, repairing furniture for hotels along Bole Road. In 2014, the founder left a factory job producing flat-pack furniture by the container load — trading volume for the right to build things properly, by hand, one piece at a time.

We moved to a larger space, brought on artisans trained in traditional cane-weaving, and made a decision that still shapes every piece we sell: no particle board, no staples, nothing we can't repair ourselves in twenty years. The name came from that first year — a fresh start, a working promise to do things differently.

Today the workshop employs fourteen people across joinery, weaving and finishing, and the showroom on Bole Road holds a piece of everything we've ever made in production.

What we hold to

Three rules that don't bend, whatever the order

Solid wood, always

No plywood carcasses, no veneer over particle board. If a piece is wood, it is wood all the way through — which is also why it can be sanded and refinished instead of replaced.

Joined, not glued

Mortise-and-tenon joinery on every frame. It takes longer at the bench and it's the reason a Phoenix chair can take a hard-tiled Addis floor for decades.

We keep the pattern

Every piece we've made is logged by design and weave pattern, so a repair or a replacement seat matches the original — even a decade later.
